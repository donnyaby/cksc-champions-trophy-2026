Tournament Overview

Everything You Need to Know

A professional-grade tournament built for the over-35 player who still has game.

📅

December 6, 2026 · Single Day

Kickoff at 10:00 AM. Third Place & Final matches in the afternoon. Awards ceremony wraps by 5:30 PM. Fields 3 & 4, Pleasant Park, Apex, NC.

⚽

8v8 · 6 Teams · 35+ Men

Max 15 players per squad. Two groups of three compete in round-robin, followed by crossover semifinals and a championship final.

📍

Apex, North Carolina

Fields 3 & 4, Pleasant Park, Apex, NC. All players must be 35 years of age as of January 31, 2026. Photo ID required at check-in.

⏳

Match Durations

Group stage: 2 × 15-min halves, 5-min halftime. Semifinals, Third Place & Final: 2 × 20-min halves, 5-min halftime.

👔

Professional Officials

Certified professional referees and linesmen assigned to every match. Highest standard of officiating for all 15 games.

🎖

Live Stream · Semis & Final

The Semifinals and Championship Final will be live streamed on the CKSC YouTube channel. Photography and videography all day.

About CKSC Corp

Soccer. Community. Legacy.

CKSC Football Club has been a cornerstone of Triangle-area soccer since its founding in 2012. Built from a shared love of the beautiful game, CKSC has grown into one of the most respected over-35 clubs in the region.

The CKSC Champions Trophy was born from a simple belief: the passion for soccer doesn't retire at 35. After a successful inaugural tournament in 2025, the 2026 edition features 6 teams with a 8v8 format, improved facilities, live streaming, and a full-day celebration of the sport.

CKSC Corp is a 501(c)(4) social welfare organization. All event proceeds support tournament operations and community soccer initiatives in the Apex/Triangle area.
